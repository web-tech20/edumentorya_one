## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Mission
The design system embodies a forward-looking, empowering, and deeply accessible educational sanctuary. Tailored for Beninese and West African students navigating critical academic thresholds (BAC, Licence, Master), it projects dignity, academic rigor, and supportive mentorship. The tone strikes a balance between elite private tuition and intuitive tech empowerment: structured yet compassionate, aspirational yet distraction-free.

### Aesthetic Movement: Contemporary African EdTech Minimalism
The visual language merges crisp digital modernism with refined organic ergonomics. The interface rejects unnecessary visual noise in favor of breathing room, gentle curves, and deliberate high-contrast typographic balance. The signature teal-to-violet gradient draws directly from the brand crest, visually framing the journey from studious analytical inquiry (teal) to enlightened academic achievement (violet). 

### Emotional Impact
- **Clarity & Calm:** Reducing pre-exam cognitive load through disciplined hierarchy, crisp surfaces, and spacious content grouping.
- **Prestige & Credibility:** Evoking the seriousness of university ambition without feeling intimidating or archaic.
- **Relational Warmth:** Humanized micro-interactions, friendly curved surfaces, and clear feedback loops that feel like 1-on-1 personal mentorship.

## Colors

### Color Architecture
- **Primary Teal (`#015F67`):** Represents structure, precision, clarity, and primary actionable states. Used for primary CTAs, active navigation items, progress rings, and key interactive highlights.
- **Secondary Violet (`#82447E`):** Represents mentorship, achievement, inspiration, and AI-driven intelligence. Used for AI mentor dialogues, mastery badges, milestone highlights, and secondary callouts.
- **Deep Petrol Blue / Dark Accent (`#024154`):** The grounding anchor. Replaces standard harsh blacks to deliver a softer, rich contrast for headers, primary typography, and structural borders in high-emphasis cards.
- **Deep Violet (`#5E3473`):** Darker counterpart to the secondary tone, reserved for subtle gradients, badge borders, and hover states of violet UI elements.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FFFFFF` (pure white for cards, sheets, dialogs).
  - `Surface Canvas`: `#F5F7F7` (soft cool tinted background preventing eye strain during long study sessions).
- **Typography & Boundaries:**
  - `Text Primary`: `#024154`
  - `Text Secondary`: `#5E6B70`
  - `Border Subtle`: `#D9E1E2`
- **Signature Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #015F67 0%, #82447E 100%)` for hero banners, AI mentor chat triggers, celebration banners, and premium plan signifiers.

### Contrast & Financial Readability
All textual pairings exceed WCAG 2.1 AA standards. Currency values displayed in FCFA (e.g., `500 FCFA`, `1 000 FCFA`, `15 000 FCFA`) utilize `#024154` at medium-to-bold weights with non-breaking thin spaces before the currency symbol to eliminate ambiguity across mobile viewports.

## Typography

### Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headings (Poppins):** Brings architectural geometry and approachable confidence to the academic platform. Its open counters and geometric structure instill optimism and forward momentum.
- **Body & Functional UI (Inter):** Highly legible, engineered for multi-density screens. Provides flawless optical rendering in dense quiz scenarios, exam question papers, formula explanations, and AI dialogue flows.

### Editorial & Cultural Standards
- Academic acronyms (BAC, SVT, PC, UAC) maintain optical alignment using small-caps or tracking adjustments (`letter-spacing: 0.02em`).
- Monetary representations always render in tabular or standard Inter numerals with a narrow non-breaking space before the currency tag: `2 500 FCFA`.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Structure
- **Mobile First Grid:** 4-column responsive grid with `16px` margins and `16px` gutters. Critical actions remain anchored within comfortable thumb-zones at the screen bottom.
- **Tablet (600px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `32px` margins and `24px` gutters, allowing side-by-side splits for study material on the left and the AI Mentor thread on the right.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid capped at a max-width container of `1280px` with `48px` page margins. Centers the educational dashboard and separates navigation rail, workspace, and context-aware mentor drawer.

### Spacing Philosophy
An 8pt dynamic scale drives all dimensional layouts (`space-xs` through `space-3xl`). Generous interior padding inside exam containers, quiz modules, and cards prevents cognitive fatigue, creating an uncluttered, premium learning workspace.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification with Tinted Ambient Depth
Depth is created through clean white card surfaces resting upon the `#F5F7F7` canvas, accented by ultra-soft, teal-tinted ambient drop shadows rather than muddy gray shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Canvas background (`#F5F7F7`) and inactive structural surfaces.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Structural Units):** `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(2, 65, 84, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(2, 65, 84, 0.03); border: 1px solid #D9E1E2;`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Floating Panels):** `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(2, 65, 84, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(2, 65, 84, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, and Mentor Assist Sheets):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(2, 65, 84, 0.16);`
- **AI Signature Elevation:** Mentor components feature a soft ambient violet glow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(130, 68, 126, 0.15);`

## Shapes

### Form Geometry
The system uses rounded geometry to create a warm, non-hostile academic environment:
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Standardized at `10px` (`rounded-xl` token) for a tactile, ergonomic touch target.
- **Content Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `16px` (`rounded-2xl` token) to establish soft separation from the canvas.
- **Badges & Subject Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to maintain immediate visual differentiation from actionable rectangular cards.
- **Chat Bubbles:** `16px` on exterior corners, with the message anchor corner pinched to `4px`.

## Components

### Iconography
- Consistent **2px linear stroke weight** with smooth rounded joins.
- Icon colors inherit contextual roles: `#015F67` for primary navigation, `#82447E` for mentor prompts, and `#5E6B70` for utility actions.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#015F67` fill with white text, `10px` border radius, `h-12` (48px) touch height on mobile. Hover state shifts to `#024154`.
- **Secondary (Mentor Action):** Linear gradient (`#015F67` to `#82447E`) with pure white text and a soft violet glow on hover.
- **Outline / Tertiary:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` solid `#D9E1E2` border, `#024154` text. Hover brings `#F5F7F7` background fill.

### Cards
- **Base Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` fill, `16px` border radius, `1px solid #D9E1E2`, with Level 1 elevation. Internal padding defaults to `20px` mobile, `24px` desktop.
- **Course / Module Card:** Includes a top accent stripe or subject tag, exam category (e.g., "BAC Série D"), and progress ring in `#015F67`.
- **Pricing & Plan Card:** Prominently highlights the subscription cost formatted as `[Number] FCFA` with a subtle teal/violet gradient border on selected/featured tiers.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height `48px`, `10px` border radius, `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #D9E1E2`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#015F67` with a `3px` focus ring of `rgba(1, 95, 103, 0.15)`.
- Label typography is anchored in `label-md` using `#024154`.

### AI Mentor Chat & Dialogue
- **Student Bubble:** `#015F67` fill, white text, right-aligned, `16px` radius with bottom-right corner at `4px`.
- **AI Mentor Bubble:** `#F5F7F7` fill with a `1px solid #D9E1E2` border, `#024154` text, left-aligned, accompanied by the stylized avatar inspired by the brand crest.
- **Action Suggestion Chips:** Soft `#82447E` tint fill (`rgba(130, 68, 126, 0.08)`), text `#82447E`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), `1px solid rgba(130, 68, 126, 0.2)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Height `28px`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), horizontal padding `12px`.
- Subject tags use light contextual tints (e.g., Mathématiques in teal tint, Philosophie in violet tint).

### Progress Indicators
- Progress bars and radial mastery rings use a `6px` stroke width with a background track of `#D9E1E2` and an active indicator filled with the signature gradient (`#015F67` to `#82447E`).
